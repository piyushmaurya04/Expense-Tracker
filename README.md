# Expense Tracker

Expense Tracker is a personal finance app designed to help users manage their money in a simple and organized way. It gives people a clear view of their spending, income, and savings habits in one place.

## Features

### 1. User Account System

- Sign up and log in securely
- Access personal finance data through a private account
- Protected pages for logged-in users only

### 2. Dashboard Overview

- See a quick summary of financial activity
- Get a snapshot of income, expenses, and balance at a glance
- Keep track of the most important numbers without digging through records

### 3. Expense Management

- Add daily expenses with details
- View all recorded expenses in one place
- Edit or remove expense entries when needed
- Keep spending history organized and easy to review

### 4. Income Management

- Record income from salary, freelance work, or other sources
- Track all earnings in a structured manner
- Review income history whenever needed
- Maintain a complete picture of cash flow

### 5. Budget Tracking

- Set or monitor monthly budget goals
- Compare planned spending with actual spending
- Stay in control of personal finances and avoid overspending

### 6. Financial Analytics

- View spending trends and patterns
- Understand where money is being used most
- Gain better insight into saving and budgeting decisions
- Make smarter financial choices based on clear summaries

### 7. Profile Management

- Manage personal account details
- Keep user information updated easily
- Maintain a personalized finance experience

### 8. Clean and User-Friendly Experience

- Simple navigation for everyday use
- Easy access to income, expense, and budget features
- Modern interface with a comfortable viewing experience

### 9. Theme Customization

- Switch between different visual themes
- Personalize the app look based on user preference

## Project Goal

The goal of Expense Tracker is to make personal financial management easier, more transparent, and more motivating. It helps users stay aware of their spending habits, track recurring income, and build better money decisions over time.

## Why It Is Useful

This app is ideal for people who want to:

- keep track of monthly expenses
- monitor earnings regularly
- stay within a budget
- understand spending habits
- manage personal finances more confidently
